# Gabriel Bisco Reinato — Professional Profile

## Professional Summary

Gabriel Bisco Reinato is an **AI & Automation Engineer** with a foundation in mechanical engineering, systems integration, and software development. His background spans end-to-end delivery of building automation projects, cloud-based data and AI workflows, and the development of full-stack AI applications. He combines hands-on experience in controls, networking, and real-world system deployment with software engineering, machine learning, and LLM integration.

Gabriel approaches technical problems from a systems perspective: understanding the underlying requirements, connecting the necessary components, building and testing the solution, and supporting its use in practice. He is pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Johns Hopkins University.

## Professional Background

Gabriel began his professional engineering career at **ATS Automation / ATS Waypoint**, delivering building management and direct digital control (DDC) systems. His responsibilities included reviewing specifications, designing and programming controls, configuring networks, coordinating equipment and contractors, troubleshooting integrations, commissioning systems, and training customers. His project work covered healthcare, hospitality, and educational facilities.

He subsequently worked with **Manhattan Investimentos (MHT Private)** on AI and workflow automation. In a small organization without an established AI infrastructure, he configured the supporting Microsoft environment and built a recurring pipeline for data collection, database storage, processing, predictive analysis, LLM-assisted reporting, and delivery to stakeholders. The implementation combined Python services, Azure Functions, Microsoft SQL, Power Automate, GitHub Actions, SharePoint, and Microsoft identity and communication services.

His independent software projects extend this experience into AI-enabled web applications, language-model workflows, machine learning, and computer vision. These projects include full-stack application development, service orchestration, model integration, automated testing, and cloud deployment.
